Car Care

Tips to Keep You Running All Four Seasons

Simple habits that save you money and breakdowns. Tap a season to learn more.

Ellensburg winters are hard on vehicles. Test your battery (cold cranking drops capacity fast), switch to winter-rated washer fluid, check tire tread and pressure, and have diesel owners verify their block heater and use anti-gel fuel additive on the coldest nights.

Wash off road salt and grime (especially the undercarriage), check brakes for winter wear, inspect wiper blades, and get an alignment if pothole season left your steering pulling.

Heat is the #1 cause of summer breakdowns. Have your cooling system and coolant tested, check A/C performance early, inspect belts and hoses for cracks, and don't ignore tire pressure as temperatures climb.

Fall is the smart time to get ahead of winter: oil change, battery test, heater/defroster check, and a full fluid top-off so the first cold snap doesn't catch you off guard.
