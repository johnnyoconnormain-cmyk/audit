Car care

A few things that save Ellensburg drivers money and roadside headaches. Tap a season for the short version.

Cold kills weak batteries — have yours tested before it strands you. Check tire tread and pressure, run winter washer fluid, and if you drive a diesel, plug in the block heater and use an anti-gel additive on the coldest nights.

Wash the winter salt and grime off, especially underneath. Have the brakes looked at after a hard winter, replace tired wiper blades, and get an alignment checked if pothole season left the steering pulling.

Heat is what breaks down on the way over the pass. Get the cooling system and coolant checked, make sure the A/C is actually cold, and look over belts and hoses for cracks before a road trip.

Best time to get ahead of winter: oil change, battery test, and make sure the heater and defroster work before the first cold morning, not during it.
